BIO
Jeremy Pagirsky is a New York based electroacoustic composer. He is currently finishing his MFA in Sonic Arts at Brooklyn College. He creates music that prioritizes the physical properties of sound, psychoacoustics, and the harmonic series. His thesis implements these principles through durational pieces that utilize bowed guitar ensembles and computers in multichannel audio settings.

Jeremy also earned an MA in Behavioral Neuroscience from Queens College. During his time there, he pursued research in pharmacology with Dr. Richard Bodnar and with Dr. Gavril Pasternak at Memorial Sloan-Kettering Cancer Center. Afterwards, he worked at the Center for HIV Educational Studies and Training (CHEST) and Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai.
